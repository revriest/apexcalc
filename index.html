3D Print Farm Rules of Thumb

Machine Depreciation: Desktop FDM printers depreciate at ~$0.40/hour over a 3,000-hour operating lifespan.

Failure Buffer: Factor 8%–12% risk buffer to account for support waste and bed detachments.

3D Print Cost Formula

Total Cost = (Filament_g × $/g) + (kWh × Rate) + (Hours × Wear/hr) + Labor

Hyperfocal Principle

Focusing at hyperfocal distance yields acceptable sharpness from half that distance to infinity.

Hyperfocal Formula

H = (focal_length² / (aperture × CoC)) + focal_length

FAQs

What is Circle of Confusion?
The maximum blur point size rendered on a sensor that still appears sharp to the human eye.

Epley vs Brzycki

Epley scales linearly; Brzycki adjusts for higher rep sets under 10 reps.

1RM Formula

Epley: Weight × (1 + Reps/30) | Brzycki: Weight × (36 / (37 - Reps))

How accurate is 1RM estimation?
